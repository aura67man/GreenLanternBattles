# Last One Standing

A text-based survival roleplay simulator in one self-contained `index.html` (vanilla JS, Canvas, no libraries). Open it in any modern browser.

Pick a world, enter your cast (names + genders), and watch randomly generated fights, deaths, alliances, romances and betrayals play out day by day, ending in a victor or a happy, bittersweet or tragic ending.

Worlds: The Hunger Games, The Maze Runner, Attack on Titan, Squid Game, The Walking Dead, Stranger Things, The Last of Us, Game of Thrones, Alice in Borderland, Jurassic Park.

Fan-made; all franchises belong to their respective owners.

# LUMEN: Constructs of Will

A neon roguelite arena game in one self-contained file: [`lumen.html`](lumen.html). It uses Canvas and vanilla JS with no libraries, and all audio and music are synthesized live with WebAudio. Open it in any modern desktop browser.

You carry a ring of light against the Hollow. You shoot with the mouse, and your main weapon is **drawing shapes**: hold right mouse (or `E`) and time slows while you draw. The shape you draw becomes a construct of hard light:

| Gesture | Construct |
|---|---|
| Straight line starting on yourself | **Lance**: piercing beam |
| Straight line elsewhere | **Bulwark**: wall that blocks enemies and bullets |
| Loop around yourself | **Aegis**: shield bubble |
| Loop around enemies | **Prison**: roots, burns, then implodes |
| Zigzag (3+ corners) | **Storm**: lightning strikes along the path |
| Curve / hook | **Scythe**: blade that races along your stroke |
| Spiral | **Vortex**: pulls enemies in, then detonates |

**Features**
- 6 Hues (classes), each with a passive and an ultimate (`Q`)
- 60 upgrade cards in 4 rarities, with construct-specific synergies, and 13 relics from bosses
- 12 enemy types and 7 elite modifiers
- 6 multi-phase bosses (every 5th sector), a final boss at Sector 30, then Endless mode
- 8 sector anomalies (Eclipse darkness, Blood Moon, Rift Storm, Bounty, …)
- 3 difficulties, combo scoring, ember currency, Sanctum permanent upgrades, 20 feats
- Codex with animated gesture guide and bestiary, settings, and progress saved in localStorage

**Controls:** WASD move · Left mouse fire · Right mouse / E draw · Space dash · Q ultimate · Esc pause
