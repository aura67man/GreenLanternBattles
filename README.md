# Last One Standing

A text-based survival roleplay simulator in one self-contained `index.html` (vanilla JS, Canvas, no libraries). Open it in any modern browser.

Pick a world, enter your cast (names + genders), and watch randomly generated fights, deaths, alliances, romances and betrayals play out day by day, ending in a victor or a happy, bittersweet or tragic ending.

Worlds: The Hunger Games, The Maze Runner, Attack on Titan, Squid Game, The Walking Dead, Stranger Things, The Last of Us, Game of Thrones, Alice in Borderland, Jurassic Park.

Fan-made; all franchises belong to their respective owners.

## Royal Chip Casino (`casino.html`)

A play-money casino in one self-contained `casino.html` (vanilla JS, Canvas, no libraries). You start with $1,000 in chips; lose them all and you start over.

Games: Blackjack, Casino Hold'em, Lucky Sevens slots, Roulette, Craps, Rocket Crash and Baccarat. Each game has a rules screen before you sit down, and you can reopen it from the RULES button.

## Krillion (`krillion.html`)

A recreation of the Krillion daily trivia dive in one self-contained `krillion.html` (vanilla JS, Canvas, no libraries). Name something for each prompt; the rarer your answer, the deeper your krill dives (10 m per point).

- **Daily Dive:** 7 prompts, the same for everyone that UTC day, 20 seconds each. Streaks and your best depth are saved in the browser.
- **Tiers:** Plankton 10, Too Clever 15, Schooler 30, Rare 60, Deep Cut 85, and one secret "One in a Krillion" answer per prompt worth 100.
- **Unknown answers:** if your answer isn't on a prompt's list, a red warning appears under the box and you can try again while the clock keeps running.
- **Endless Dive** (three passes or timeouts and you're out) and **Themed Packs** (food, nature, world, culture, science).
- 50 prompts with about 12,000 accepted answers, including spelling variants and small typos.
- Share your dive as an emoji grid.

Answer rarity comes from built-in answer lists ordered from most to least common, not real player data. Fan-made; not affiliated with the original game.
