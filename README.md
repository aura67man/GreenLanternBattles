# Last One Standing

A text-based survival roleplay simulator in one self-contained `index.html` (vanilla JS, Canvas, no libraries). Open it in any modern browser.

Pick a world, enter your cast (names + genders), and watch randomly generated fights, deaths, alliances, romances and betrayals play out day by day, ending in a victor or a happy, bittersweet or tragic ending.

Worlds: The Hunger Games, The Maze Runner, Attack on Titan, Squid Game, The Walking Dead, Stranger Things, The Last of Us, Game of Thrones, Alice in Borderland, Jurassic Park.

Fan-made; all franchises belong to their respective owners.

# Game Show Hub

`game-show-hub.html` is a separate single-file game (vanilla JS, Canvas rendering, no libraries). Open it in any modern browser to play eight TV game shows, each styled after its show:

| Show | Modes | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Jeopardy! | Solo / VS 2 AI (buzzer race) | Easy · Medium · Hard (clue sets, values, AI buzz speed) |
| Who Wants to Be a Millionaire | Solo / VS AI (alternating hot seat) | Easy · Medium · Hard (question sets, lifeline reliability) |
| Deal or No Deal | Solo vs the Banker | — (pure luck) |
| Wheel of Fortune | Solo (3 strikes) / VS 2 AI, plus Bonus Round | Easy · Medium · Hard (puzzles, AI skill, bonus rules) |
| Family Feud | Solo / VS AI family, plus Fast Money | Easy · Medium · Hard (surveys, AI skill, FM clock) |
| The Price Is Right | Solo / VS 3 AI (Contestants' Row), Plinko, Cliff Hangers, Clock Game, Big Wheel, Showcase | Easy · Medium · Hard |
| Press Your Luck | Solo / VS 2 AI | Easy · Medium · Hard (questions, light speed, Whammy count) |
| The Chase | VS the Chaser | Easy · Medium · Hard (questions, Chaser speed & accuracy) |

Typed answers (Jeopardy!, Feud, Wheel solves) are matched leniently: "What is…" is optional and small typos are forgiven.
